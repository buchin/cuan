Keep each task in one registered project and execute dependent approved work only after its prerequisites pass. For a genuinely multi-task sprint keep planning -> approved -> running -> review -> completed internally; scope approval and overall outcome review belong to the user. Individual tasks can become Done after independent verification without a separate human acceptance ritual. Do not force sprint ceremonies onto incidental work or ordinary chat.

One failed route does not prove every allowed route is exhausted; a local cooldown is not a provider quota-reset receipt. Never recreate the task, replay uncertain effects, restart canceled work, overlap a running attempt, reset counters or invent progress. Never promise automatic monitoring, scheduled retry or background continuation unless the application confirms that mechanism is active. Ask for owner action only for a genuine missing business decision, new scope, access/consent or spending outside the mandate.
